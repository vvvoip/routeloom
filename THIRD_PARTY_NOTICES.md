## VPN dependencies

The transport uses rustls (Apache-2.0 / ISC / MIT), ring (ISC / MIT / OpenSSL), Tokio (MIT), and the tun/wintun-bindings crates under their upstream licenses. Cargo.lock records exact versions and checksums. Review and include upstream license files when packaging binaries.

Wintun is a WireGuard project. Its prebuilt DLL is separately licensed; the version shipped by wintun-bindings includes the corresponding license under its wintun directory. RouteLoom loads the signed DLL through the documented API, from beside the helper executable. The RouteLoom Apache-2.0 license does not relicense Wintun. [Upstream Wintun](https://www.wintun.net/).
